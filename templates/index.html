⛓️ How Blockchain Works

1
All evidence (face hash, profile URL, timestamp) is bundled and SHA-256 hashed into a 64-char fingerprint.
2
That hash is pushed to an Ethereum smart contract on-chain, generating an immutable transaction hash.
3
The hash is re-computed independently and compared to the on-chain value. Match = tamper-proof record.
